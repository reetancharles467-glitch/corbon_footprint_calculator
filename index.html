1 / 8

Human activities create emissions

Cars, power stations, factories, kitchens and landfills: ordinary life runs on burning carbon. Every habit you are about to measure starts here.

2 / 8

Carbon dioxide accumulates

CO₂ does not simply disappear. Much of it stays in the air for centuries, and its concentration has climbed from about 280 ppm before industry to over 420 ppm today.

3 / 8

The greenhouse effect

Sunlight reaches the surface and warms it. Greenhouse gases then hold back some of the outgoing heat, like a blanket that keeps getting thicker.

4 / 8

Earth heats up

The planet is now more than 1.1 °C warmer than in the late 1800s. Small averages hide big shifts in oceans, forests and weather.

5 / 8

The ice gives way

Glaciers and ice sheets are shrinking, and meltwater adds to rising seas that threaten coasts and the people who live on them.

6 / 8

Climate disasters

Heatwaves, drought, wildfire, floods and stronger storms are becoming more intense. This is the cost of an unchecked footprint.
